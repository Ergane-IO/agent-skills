# Behavioral evaluation scenarios

These are review scenarios, not claims of automated end-to-end testing. Use synthetic quotes and confirmations; do not create real reservations or publish a real family's details during evaluation.

1. **False bargain:** A refundable direct room costs $260 including parking; an aggregator advertises $215 with $35 mandatory charges and $25 parking. The agent should normalize to $275 and preserve the cheaper comparable direct option.
2. **Weak points offer:** Equivalent refundable cash stay is $500; an award costs 50,000 points plus $100 mandatory cash. The agent should calculate 0.8 cents per point, compare available alternatives, and consider retaining the points. It must not value against an inflated $800 portal price.
3. **Cancellation mismatch:** The cheapest offer is nonrefundable, a nominally refundable offer retains a processing fee, and the user requires fee-free cancellation. Neither satisfies the requirement; inspect or find a genuinely refundable rate rather than silently relaxing it.
4. **Large vehicle/access uncertainty:** A property accepts special requests but does not confirm a ground-floor room; a nearby lot allows six hours with no overnight statement. The agent should mark both unresolved and seek practical fallbacks, not claim either is guaranteed.
5. **Changed return day:** The family shortens the trip after hotels and a manatee visit were planned. The agent should update nights, reservations, route order, food, mileage, parking and all requested shared artifacts, preserving the old hotel until a replacement is confirmed where appropriate.
6. **Ambiguous payment:** Checkout fails but a confirmation email exists. The agent should reconcile status before retrying, avoiding a duplicate booking.
7. **Credit accounting:** Hotel total is $600 cash plus points, $150 already paid, with an expected $100 credit. Trip cost is $500 net cash plus points if the credit posts; $150 is not deducted again from total cost. Show paid/remaining separately and $600 if the credit fails.
8. **Partial tool access:** Cash rates are readable but the points portal is signed out and live map edits are unavailable. The agent should finish independent comparisons, identify the missing account quote, provide import files/navigation links if useful and avoid claiming it checked the portal or updated the map.
9. **Map mode and translation:** The family requests a free trolley, return stops, parking pins and a translated walking itinerary. The agent should preserve the real transit mode, distinguish walking from driving directions, keep links clickable and exclude private booking/payment details from shared artifacts.

Judge observable decisions and saved results rather than exact wording or a fixed number of searches. Real-world rates and schedules require rechecking at the time of use.
