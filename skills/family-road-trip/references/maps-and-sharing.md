# Maps, directions and shared itineraries

## Structure and points

Inspect the existing map, edit permissions, layers and sharing. Reuse its identity/link and preserve unrelated descriptions. Work within the platform's current layer limit by merging or reusing layers.

Useful toggleable groups: outbound/return and day-trip routes; hotels and core destinations; parking; optional sights; food by day/area; local transit and walking loops. Adapt grouping to the trip. Hide/remove canceled routes from the final view; keep alternatives only if clearly labeled. Distinguish booked stays from candidates.

When the user is comparing bases, show their geographic relationship to the actual sights and day trips, including the daily driving/parking consequences. After a base is selected, make the final view clear instead of leaving every candidate equally prominent. A food overlay should offer alternatives along the route, not imply every restaurant is a planned meal.

Place pins at the actual visitor entrance, boarding point, parking entrance or restaurant branch, not a city center or similarly named location. Verify coordinates/addresses. Relevant descriptions should contain date/visit window, optional status, what to do, duration/walking effort, cost/booking status, official links and parking/transit instructions.

Separate hotel entrance from vehicle access, and park admission from tour check-in/trailhead. Parking pins should explain clearance/size limits, overnight rules, fees, in/out access, reservation status and fallback. Do not put private confirmation codes or health details on a shared map.

## Routes

- Prefer navigable directions layers or Google Maps directions links. Use the correct mode: a driving route is not a walking loop or bus route. Divide long trips into useful day segments and respect waypoint limits.
- After CSV/KML import, verify geocoding, coordinates, labels, categories, styling and descriptions. Explicit coordinates help prevent ambiguous addresses.
- Label approximate lines as schematic. Never represent them as verified sidewalks, roads or transit alignments.
- Transit guidance needs correct direction/headsign, outbound/return stops, transfers, fares, operating hours and live-arrival links. Use authoritative route data/GTFS where available; check service validity and recheck near departure.
- Walking loops need a short option, moving time versus family elapsed time, rest points, dinner detours, daylight/access constraints and a clickable directions link. Do not label a route accessible merely because a mapping engine provides a walking line.

## Sharing and final checks

Honor the requested audience. Set link-viewer access when requested and verify it; do not broaden private access by inference. State when sharing could not be verified.

Inspect the saved map for final hotels/dates, route order, the return leg, parking, selected day trips, new layers and disabled old routes. A local import file or import preview does not prove the live map changed. If editing is unavailable, deliver an import-ready file and route links and state that the map remains unchanged.

Keep extensive text, translations and booking checklists in the itinerary document and cross-link it with the map. Preserve existing links and requested language. Follow applicable tool requirements for screenshots or edit proof; do not use an unchanged viewer as proof of a write.

For a family walking evening, assemble a coherent short loop from the hotel with rest/dinner options and a shorter return, not just isolated POIs. Check that markets/events occur on the actual weekday; seasonal decoration or an exterior landmark can be optional without implying an event is scheduled.
