# Multi-platform price hunting and points strategy

## Establish comparable searches

Recover exact dates/year, travelers/children's ages, rooms/beds, bathrooms, access needs, vehicle/parking, cancellation requirements and location tolerance. Search the same occupancy everywhere. Check whether two rooms beat a suite or rental only when both layouts suit the family. Nearby bases and date shifts are alternatives to assess within the user's flexibility, not silent changes to their trip.

Build a short property shortlist, then compare channels for the same property/room and alternative properties in suitable areas. A metasearch result is a lead; open the actual seller's rate and terms. Search these channel types when relevant and available:

- Hotel/property direct, including a freely available member rate when eligible.
- Cash aggregators: Booking.com and a relevant independent alternative such as Expedia, Hotels.com, Priceline or a regional equivalent. Avoid claiming comprehensive coverage from multiple brands showing the same underlying inventory.
- Rentals: Airbnb, Vrbo or appropriate local property managers, including cleaning/service fees, deposits, minimum stays, parking and check-in friction.
- User's eligible card portal, such as Chase Travel, for cash, points and mixed payment. Logged-in offers may differ from public listings; public search is not evidence of account-specific pricing.
- Hotel loyalty awards and eligible transfer partners when the user's currencies, inventory and likely value make them plausible. First check availability and transfer rules; no speculative transfer.

Match room category and cancellation conditions. Mark genuine differences: breakfast, resort charges, parking, layout, taxes, payment timing, loyalty earnings/benefits, accessibility guarantee. A cheaper nonrefundable room is not the winner when free cancellation is required.

## Find discounts that actually apply

Check direct/member offers, existing aggregator status such as Genius, eligible mobile/app rates, official promo codes, card credits/offers and legitimate cashback when material. Do not sign up for paid memberships or use discounts the user is ineligible for. Do not invent coupon codes or count a coupon until its current terms and applicability are verified; distinguish an advertised code from a discount actually accepted at checkout.

Verify stacking restrictions, dates, minimum spend, room exclusions, prepay requirements and cancellation consequences. Track cashback, future travel credit and statement credits separately from immediate discounts. Count uncertain benefits in a conditional total, not the guaranteed price. Do not double count a portal benefit or credit across stays.

## Compare cash with points

For each competitive award capture points, cash copay, taxes/fees, parking, refund deadline, redeposit rules and likely timing of returned points. Points-booked does not automatically mean free cancellation. A refund to the loyalty account is different from refunding money or reversing a transfer.

Use the lowest genuinely comparable acceptable cash rate as the reference, not an inflated portal headline price. Effective value in cents per point:

`100 × (comparable cash total − award cash total) / points redeemed`

Show how eligible credits change the decision, counting each only where it can actually be used. Compare the result with the user's available alternative redemptions or chosen valuation; do not invent a universal threshold. If their points have a cash-out option, include that opportunity cost where relevant and verified. Do not force use of all points. A low-cash option, a best-value option and saving points can be distinct recommendations.

For transfer awards, verify exact room inventory, transfer ratio, timing, any active bonus, cancellation/redeposit fees and irreversibility. Recheck before committing. Include cash fees that portals or awards leave due at the property.

## Deliver a decision, not a pile of search results

Use a compact table for finalists:

| Stay / exact room | Channel | Cash total including mandatory fees | Points | Parking / breakfast | Free-cancel deadline | Net after verified discounts / conditional credits | Checked / link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

State which channels were checked, which were unavailable/login-blocked, and the last verification time. Keep cash and points in separate columns. Compare refundable and nonrefundable rates separately if asked; quantify the actual savings and risk rather than labeling both as interchangeable.

Recommend the best fit with its cash/points tradeoff, real savings against the runner-up and unresolved conditions. Explain if a cheaper northern/outlying base increases driving, tolls, parking or walking burden. Stop expanding the search when additional channels no longer change the decision; do not claim to have searched the whole market.

Refresh shortlisted totals and final terms immediately before booking. If the user already has a refundable stay, compare net savings after any cancellation cost and confirm replacement before canceling. Offer a later recheck when useful; monitor or schedule it only if requested.
