# Reservations, points and budget

## Comparable, bookable offers

Use exact dates/year, nights, all travelers and children's ages. Verify maximum occupancy, beds and the specific room/rate plan. A two-adult search is not evidence that a room accommodates five.

For serious offers record channel/link, checked time, room, total with taxes/mandatory fees, due-now versus due-property amounts, parking, breakfast, payment timing and cancellation deadline/time zone/penalty. A refundable deposit and cancellation penalty are different facts.

For free cancellation verify refundability of taxes, processing/platform fees and deposits too. If terms are missing or contradictory, mark unresolved; do not book on an assumption. Recheck final checkout terms before committing.

Confirm replacement lodging before canceling the old reservation when appropriate. Capture completed cancellation and expected refund separately; a pending refund is not received cash. After payment errors inspect trips/confirmation/email before retrying. Stop blind retries after repeated failure and use a supported alternative or handoff.

## Points and credits

- Show points separately from cash; do not call a points-funded stay free or subtract its value twice.
- Compare equivalent cancellable offers. Approximate cents per point: `100 × (comparable cash total − award cash total) / points used`, including mandatory award fees. Explain differences between the reference offers.
- Verify current balance, actual card/product redemption offers and eligibility. Do not assume a fixed Chase rate, expiration policy, transfer value or promotional boost.
- Verify which cash charges qualify for a credit and whether points reduce that amount. Count each credit once; distinguish expected from posted and show the total without an uncertain credit.
- Order booking steps around actual benefit rules and inventory risk; do not invent a required credit sequence. Preserve the user's cancellation requirement while they are deciding.
- Before transferring points verify inventory, full charges and transfer terms; transfers may be irreversible. Do not transfer speculatively.

## One reconciled budget

Use a compact ledger (see [trip-record.md](trip-record.md)) and calculate with code or a calculator. Distinguish booked actuals, quotes and estimates. Align dates, headcount and vehicle with the itinerary.

Include:

1. Accommodation cash, points, mandatory due-property fees and eligible credits. Deposits already included in totals are not additional costs. Remove canceled stays from retained trip cost; track refund cash flow separately.
2. Food by day or family allowance: breakfasts included in hotels, groceries/snacks, tax and tips. Restaurant pins are choices, not meals to purchase at every pin.
3. Fuel: route miles plus local driving/detours, vehicle MPG and fuel-price assumption. `miles / MPG × price per gallon`; for EVs use consumption and charging rates. Distinguish estimates from actual charges.
4. Parking, tolls, transit and app/reservation fees. Avoid counting hotel-included parking again.
5. Activities: adult/child tickets, taxes/booking fees, admission, passes and required reservations. Verify pass coverage for each park, concession activity and current residency rules. Federal passes do not automatically cover state parks or private tours.
6. Optional costs and a stated contingency; say whether each is included or an add-on.

`trip cash = retained lodging cash + mandatory due-property charges + food + fuel + parking/tolls/transit + activities + included optional costs + contingency − eligible credits`

Report total cash and points separately. Distinguish already paid, remaining due and credits/refunds pending when evidence supports that breakdown. Already paid amounts remain part of total trip cost.

Use cents for confirmed charges and rounded planning allowances. Give a target and useful range, with assumptions behind uncertainties. Do not present future parking or fuel estimates as guaranteed prices.

When dates, lodging, routes or activities change, recompute affected nights, meals, mileage, parking and tickets. Update requested plan summaries and translations that contain the budget; replace stale totals. Verify arithmetic, cash/points split, single application of credits and no duplicate mandatory fees.
